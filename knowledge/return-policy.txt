ZeeCart Return Policy

Last updated: January 2026. This policy applies to all products sold by ZeeCart (a fictional company used for demonstration purposes).

1. Return window
You can request a return within 10 days of the delivery date. This applies to all electronics and wearables sold by ZeeCart, including audio products, computer accessories, mobile accessories, office electronics, and smart watches. The 10 days start on the day the order status changes to "delivered", and the exact last day for each order is shown on its order page. Return requests made after the 10 days are not accepted, except for warranty claims (see the Warranty Policy).

2. Eligible products
Most products can be returned if they are:
- Unused, or only tested briefly to check that they work.
- In their original packaging, with all accessories, manuals, and cables included.
- Accompanied by the order number.
Eligible categories include keyboards, mice, laptop stands, USB-C hubs, Bluetooth speakers, webcams, power banks, smart watches, and over-ear headphones.

3. Non-returnable products
For hygiene and safety reasons the following cannot be returned unless they arrive defective or damaged:
- In-ear products such as wireless earphones and earbuds, once the sealed packaging or hygiene seal has been opened.
- Products marked "Final Sale" or "Clearance" at the time of purchase.
- Gift cards and digital products.
- Products with removed serial numbers or missing parts.

4. Damaged or defective products
If your product arrives damaged, defective, or is not the item you ordered, report it within 7 days of delivery. Include your order number and clear photos of the product and the packaging. In these cases ZeeCart pays the return shipping and you receive a full refund or a free replacement. Items under ₹500 that arrive damaged do not need to be sent back.

5. Used products
Products that show clear signs of use, such as scratches, stains, worn keycaps, or a drained battery from heavy use, may be refunded only partially. After inspection, the refund may be reduced by up to 50% of the product price. Products damaged by the customer cannot be returned.

6. Customer responsibility
Customers are responsible for:
- Requesting the return within the 10-day window.
- Packing the product securely, ideally in its original box.
- Sending the product back within 10 days after the return is approved.
- Return shipping costs for change-of-mind returns. A flat ₹99 return label fee is deducted from the refund when you use a ZeeCart prepaid label.
- Removing personal data from devices such as smart watches.

7. How to request a return and approval process
1. Open Your Orders on the ZeeCart website and find the delivered order. While the return window is open, the order shows a "Return items" button and the last day to return.
2. Choose the reason for the return: defective or not working, arrived damaged, wrong item received, not as described, no longer needed, or found a better price. Add a comment if you wish, then confirm.
3. The order status changes to "return_requested". Orders whose return window has closed show no return button.
4. Support checks the request and replies within 1 business day with a Return Authorization number (RMA) and return instructions.
5. Send the product back within 10 days of approval.
6. The warehouse inspects the product within 3 business days of receiving it.
7. The refund is processed as described in the Refund Policy.
Only one return request can be made per order. In-ear products such as wireless earphones can only be returned if they are defective, damaged, or the wrong item, so for those orders only these reasons are accepted.

8. Exchanges
We do not offer direct exchanges. To get a different product, return the original for a refund and place a new order. For defective items, a replacement can be arranged.
