ZeeCart Company Overview

ZeeCart is a fictional company created for demonstration purposes. The details below describe the company and are consistent with the other ZeeCart policy documents.

1. Company profile
Company: ZeeCart
Founded: 2021
Headquarters: Bengaluru, India
Business: Online ecommerce marketplace
Products: Consumer electronics, computer accessories, smart devices, and everyday technology products
Customer Support: Available Monday to Saturday, 9 AM to 8 PM IST
Website: ZeeCart is an online-only store with no physical retail outlets.

2. Leadership
ZeeCart was founded by Rajiv Mishra, who is 45 years old and serves as Founder and Chief Executive Officer. Before starting ZeeCart, Rajiv spent more than 15 years working in logistics and retail technology. He started the company in Bengaluru with the goal of making reliable consumer electronics easy to buy and easy to get support for. Rajiv still reviews customer feedback every week and is directly involved in how support issues are handled.

3. Mission
ZeeCart's mission is to make everyday technology affordable, genuine, and supported. We sell only authentic products sourced from brands and authorised distributors, and we stand behind every sale with clear return, refund, cancellation, and warranty policies.

4. Product range
ZeeCart sells products in these categories:
- Audio: wireless earphones, headphones, and Bluetooth speakers.
- Computer Accessories: mechanical keyboards, wireless mice, and USB-C hubs.
- Wearables: smart watches and fitness bands.
- Office: laptop stands and webcams.
- Mobile Accessories: power banks and chargers.
Most products carry a 12-month warranty (see the Warranty Policy).

5. Fulfilment and logistics
ZeeCart operates warehouses across India, including locations serving Bengaluru, Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Hyderabad, and Kolkata. Around 2,500 people work in our warehouse and logistics operations, handling storage, packing, quality checks, and dispatch. Orders are processed within 1 business day and shipped through partnered courier companies. Standard delivery takes 5 to 7 business days and express delivery takes 2 to 3 business days. ZeeCart delivers to most pin codes in India. See the Shipping Policy for delivery details.

6. Customer support team
ZeeCart has a customer support team of 25 people based in Bengaluru. Human agents are available Monday to Saturday, 9 AM to 8 PM IST, and are closed on Sundays and national holidays. In addition, Zee, ZeeCart's AI support assistant, is available 24 hours a day to help with order status, delivery questions, returns, cancellations, and policy questions. Complicated cases, such as damaged products, payment disputes, and long delivery delays, are handed to the human support team. See the General Support Policy for escalation rules.

7. Payments and pricing
All prices on ZeeCart are in Indian Rupees (INR) and include applicable taxes. Standard shipping is free on orders of Rs. 999 or more. Refunds are issued in INR to the original payment method, as described in the Refund Policy.

8. Values
- Genuine products: we do not sell counterfeit or grey-market goods.
- Honest communication: we tell customers the real status of their order, including when it is delayed.
- Fair policies: customers can cancel, return, and claim warranty under clear rules.
- Privacy: support staff and Zee only access the account of the customer they are helping.

9. Contact
Customers should contact support through the ZeeCart website or by chatting with Zee after logging in. Human support follows up on tickets within the response times listed in the General Support Policy.
