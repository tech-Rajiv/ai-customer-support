ZeeCart Warranty Policy

Last updated: January 2026. This policy applies to all products sold by ZeeCart (a fictional company used for demonstration purposes).

1. Warranty period
Every ZeeCart product includes a limited warranty that starts on the delivery date:
- 12 months: wireless earphones, headphones, Bluetooth speakers, smart watches, webcams, mechanical keyboards, wireless mice, USB-C hubs, and power banks.
- 6 months: laptop stands and other passive accessories.
- Batteries: covered for 12 months, and considered defective if the maximum capacity falls below 70% of the rated capacity within that period under normal use.
The warranty does not extend the return window. Returns are covered by the Return Policy, while warranty covers defects that appear later.

2. Eligible defects
The warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship that occur under normal use, including:
- Device does not power on or charge.
- Audio problems such as one earbud not working, crackling sound, or microphone failure.
- Keys, buttons, or scroll wheels that stop responding.
- Connectivity problems such as Bluetooth or USB ports that do not work.
- Screen or sensor faults on smart watches.
- Power banks that no longer charge or discharge.

3. Exclusions
The warranty does not cover:
- Physical damage such as cracks, dents, bent parts, or broken hinges.
- Liquid damage, beyond the stated water-resistance rating of the product.
- Damage from unauthorized repairs, opening the device, or modifications.
- Normal wear such as worn keycaps, scratched surfaces, or faded cables.
- Loss or theft.
- Damage caused by incompatible chargers or power sources.
- Cosmetic damage that does not affect function.
Products with removed or altered serial numbers are not covered.

4. Warranty claim process
1. Contact ZeeCart Support and give the order number, a description of the problem, and photos or a short video of the defect.
2. Support asks you to try basic troubleshooting such as resetting the device, updating firmware, or testing a different cable.
3. If the problem remains, support creates a support ticket and issues a Return Authorization (RMA) number.
4. Send the product to us within 14 days. ZeeCart pays shipping for approved claims.
5. The product is inspected within 5 business days of receipt.
6. If approved, you receive a repair or replacement. If the claim is rejected, we explain the reason and offer a paid repair or return the product at your cost.

5. Replacement vs repair
- Dead on arrival: if the product is defective within the first 30 days after delivery, we replace it with a new unit, or refund it at your choice.
- After 30 days: we first attempt a repair. Repairs are completed within 15 business days.
- If a repair is not possible or takes longer than 15 business days, we replace the product with a new or refurbished unit of equal or better specification.
- A replacement product carries the remaining warranty of the original, or 90 days, whichever is longer.
- If the product is out of stock and cannot be replaced, a refund of the purchase price is issued.
