ZeeCart Refund Policy

Last updated: January 2026. This policy applies to all refunds issued by ZeeCart (a fictional company used for demonstration purposes).

1. Refund eligibility
A customer is eligible for a refund when:
- An order is cancelled before delivery in line with the Cancellation Policy.
- A product is returned within the 30-day return window and approved by ZeeCart (see the Return Policy).
- A product arrives damaged, defective, or incorrect.
- An order is lost, or delayed beyond the limits described in the Shipping Policy and the customer chooses to cancel.
Refunds are not available for products returned after the return window, products that were damaged by the customer, or non-returnable items such as opened in-ear earphones that are not defective.

2. Refund method
Refunds are issued to the original payment method used for the purchase. We cannot refund to a different card or account. Customers may instead choose store credit, which is added to the ZeeCart account instantly and is valid for 12 months. Customers who pick store credit receive a 5% bonus.

3. Refund processing time
- After a cancellation before shipping: the refund is started immediately and appears within 3 to 5 business days.
- After a cancellation of a shipped order: the refund is started once the carrier confirms the package is returned, or after 10 business days at the latest, and appears within 3 to 5 business days after that.
- After a return: the warehouse inspects the product within 3 business days of receiving it. After approval, the refund is processed and appears within 5 to 7 business days.
- For damaged-product refunds: the refund is processed within 2 business days of approving the claim and appears within 5 to 7 business days.
Banks may need additional time to post a refund. If a refund has not appeared after 10 business days, contact support with your order number.

4. Refund after cancellation
A cancelled order is refunded in full, including taxes and shipping fees, when the cancellation is made before the order ships. For cancellations of delayed orders, the full amount is refunded including the shipping fee.

5. Refund after return
For change-of-mind returns, the refund equals the product price minus the ₹99 return label fee if you used our prepaid label. The original outbound shipping fee is not refunded unless the return is due to our error. If the returned product shows signs of use, the refund may be reduced by up to 50% after inspection, and the customer is notified by email before the refund is processed.

6. Damaged-product refunds
For damaged, defective, or incorrect items reported within 7 days of delivery, ZeeCart refunds the full product price plus the original shipping fee. The customer can choose between a refund and a free replacement. Products under ₹500 are refunded without needing to be returned.

7. Partial refunds
Partial refunds may be granted for orders with multiple items when only some items are returned or cancelled. The shipping fee is refunded only if the whole order is returned or cancelled.

8. Delay compensation
When an order is delivered more than 5 business days late, the standard shipping fee is refunded automatically. For express orders delivered more than 1 business day late, the express fee is refunded.

9. Refund status
Customers can ask support for the status of a refund at any time. Support can see whether the refund is pending, approved, or issued, and the date it was sent to the bank.
