ZeeCart Shipping Policy

Last updated: January 2026. This policy applies to all orders placed on ZeeCart (a fictional company used for demonstration purposes).

1. Order processing
Orders are processed within 1 business day of confirmation. Orders placed after 2:00 PM local time, or on weekends and public holidays, begin processing on the next business day. You will see the order status move from "pending" to "confirmed" to "processing" and then "shipped".

2. Delivery options and estimates
Standard delivery takes 5 to 7 business days after the order ships. Standard shipping is free on orders of ₹999 or more and costs ₹49 on smaller orders.
Express delivery takes 2 to 3 business days after the order ships and costs ₹149. Express delivery is not available for PO boxes or remote regions.
The expected delivery date shown on your order is an estimate, not a guarantee. It is calculated from the day your order ships and the delivery option you chose.

3. Regional delays
Deliveries to remote or rural regions, islands, and areas affected by severe weather, strikes, or natural disasters may take 2 to 4 additional business days. When we know about a regional disruption, we update the expected delivery date on your order and mark the order as "delayed".

4. Delayed shipments
An order is considered delayed when it has not been delivered by its expected delivery date. Its status is changed to "delayed" and the expected date is kept for reference.
What customers should do when an order is delayed:
- Wait at least 1 business day past the expected date, because carriers often complete deliveries late in the day.
- Check the order page for status updates.
- If the order is still not delivered, contact ZeeCart Support with your order number, or simply describe the product you ordered.

How support handles delays:
- 1 to 3 business days past the expected date: support confirms the carrier status and shares the latest information.
- More than 3 business days past the expected date: the customer may choose to keep waiting or cancel the order for a full refund (see the Cancellation Policy).
- More than 5 business days past the expected date: the standard shipping fee is refunded. For express orders, the express fee is refunded if delivery is more than 1 business day late.

5. Escalation
Support escalates a delivery issue to the carrier investigation team when:
- An order is more than 7 business days past its expected delivery date.
- The tracking shows "delivered" but the customer did not receive the package.
- The package is returned to sender or the delivery address is flagged as invalid.
- The package shows no tracking movement for 5 or more business days.
An escalation receives a first response within 24 hours and a resolution within 7 business days.

6. Lost packages
If an order is not delivered within 10 business days of its expected delivery date, it is treated as lost. ZeeCart will then either ship a replacement at no cost (subject to stock) or issue a full refund, according to the customer's choice.

7. Address changes
The delivery address can be changed only while the order is in "pending", "confirmed", or "processing" status. Once an order has shipped, the address cannot be changed by ZeeCart.

8. Failed delivery attempts
The carrier makes up to 3 delivery attempts. After the third failed attempt, the package is returned to our warehouse and a refund is issued less the original shipping cost.
